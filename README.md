## Overview

This repository contains the public manuscript files for:

**Foundations of Codex Alpha Dynamics: A Variational Derivation of an Informational Extension of Einstein Field Equations**

The manuscript presents a minimal variational formulation of the dynamical core of the Codex Alpha theoretical framework. Its purpose is not to extend the theory with additional phenomenological claims, but to isolate and derive the smallest mathematical structure capable of supporting the effective continuous dynamics of the framework.

The central field equation derived in the manuscript is:

```text
G_{μν} + Λg_{μν} = (8πG / c⁴) ⟨ T̂_{μν} ⟩_{∇𝒦}
```

where the right-hand side is defined as the metric response of a coherence-weighted functional measure rather than introduced phenomenologically.

---

## Scientific Scope

This work focuses on the **effective continuous sector** of the Telascura, the regime in which the underlying discrete informational structure of Codex Alpha can be approximated by a smooth Lorentzian manifold.

The manuscript develops:

- a minimal variational action principle;
- a scalar coherence field `𝒦`;
- a coherence-weighted Schwinger functional `W[g, ∇𝒦]`;
- dimensional consistency conditions;
- recovery of General Relativity in the uniform-coherence limit;
- falsifiability through measurable deviations from standard observables;
- absence of Ostrogradsky ghosts in the minimal continuous sector;
- applications to cosmological perturbations, gravitational waves, gravitational lensing, and post-Newtonian regimes.

Non-local, retrocausal, and fully discrete nodal effects are outside the scope of this manuscript and are treated in the broader Codex Alpha 3.0 — Iconoclast Edition.
